What the usage data says · search event log, Sep 22 to Oct 26

112 of 200 stores search every week, behind the pace for December

Use grew fast after launch, then flattened. At this rate about 130 stores search weekly by Dec 1, short of the 150 we aimed for.

  • upStores that try it stick: 9 in 10 come back the next week
  • surpriseNight shifts search three times as often as day shifts
  • data gapTwo stores' handheld scanners never report searches, so their use is invisible

Why it mattersStores that start keep going, so the gap is the stores that haven't tried it yet.

SourcesEvent log: distinct stores with a staff search, per week, Repeat use: stores active one week and also the next, Test and head-office accounts excluded
