# Report format

Plain prose, varied sentence length, no em-dashes, no hype words, no "it's not X, it's Y". Table cells short enough to read on a phone.

## The verdict (always)

1. **Heading**: "<Idea name>: verdict".
2. **Summary**: two to four sentences. What it is, how it does on the lenses, how it does on evidence, its single biggest risk.
3. **Verdict table**: Framework | Verdict | Why. Rows in this order: problem and urgency, market size, crowded but mediocre, internal-tool test, why now, adoption, free-first, business model, moat, founder fit (one row per founder), constraints, each user principle (filters and heuristics), assumption map, lean startup (with the score), Mom Test.
4. **Pros.** One paragraph opening with the bolded word. The forces it rides, why it would stick, why these founders.
5. **Cons.** One paragraph opening with the bolded word. Any red-flag principles that fired go first. Then who can kill it, who's converging, what makes it hard to adopt or sell, what could go badly wrong.
6. **Scorecard row**: Crowding | Buyer urgency | Founder fit | Time to first dollar | Scale | Platform risk | Biggest unknown.
7. **Evidence line**: "Evidence: n/10 (highest rung: level k, <what>)." Then the single cheapest next test, its pass threshold, and when to check it.

## The full battery (unless a quick read was asked for)

After the verdict, one section each, using the tables in the references: market size (with the arithmetic), competitors (the funded-players table), adoption path, assumption map, experiments, customer research findings, interview script, MVP and pivot triggers, test plan. End with open questions for the founders, each with a best guess, for anything the profile or principles didn't cover.

## A batch of ideas

Give each idea its verdict, then a ranking table: Idea | Lenses passed | Evidence score | Scorecard summary | Why this rank. Rank on lenses passed (filters weigh more than heuristics), break ties on evidence, and let real customer evidence (level 3 or higher) outrank paper passes. Run the full battery on the top idea only, and offer it for the others.
