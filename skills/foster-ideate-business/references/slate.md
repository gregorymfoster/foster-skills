# The slate

Plain prose, varied sentence length, no em-dashes, no hype words, no "it's not X, it's Y". Table cells short enough to read on a phone.

1. **Heading**: "Idea slate: <team or brief>".
2. **Summary**: two to four sentences. How many ideas were generated, how many survived, the top pick and why, and the handoff.
3. **Shortlist table**: Rank | Idea | Buyer | Lens | Proof of need | Biggest open question. Five to eight rows.
4. **One section per shortlisted idea**: the three-line frame (customer and problem, what it does, how it makes money), the wedge, the founder who leads and why, the proof with links, and the open question.
5. **Killed ideas**: a table of Idea | Kill reason, one line each. This is what stops the next run from regenerating them.
6. **Handoff**: the one to three ideas going to `foster-assess-business`, with their three-line frames ready to paste, and whether to run a quick read or the full battery.

Rank on founder fit first, then how many lenses produced the idea, then how strong and recent the proof is. Real customer signal already in hand (a buyer asking for it, a paid workaround) outranks everything on paper.
