# Quick screen

A fast keep-or-kill pass over the raw list, a minute per idea. It is not an assessment; `foster-assess-business` does that for the shortlist. Kill with a named reason, never with "not exciting".

Kill when any of these is true:

1. **No buyer.** You can't name who signs, or the user who loves it can't pay and nobody above them cares.
2. **Taken.** A polished, well-funded leader already serves this exact buyer, and there's no wedge they're structurally unable to chase (a segment too small for them, a deployment model that breaks their business, a price that would cannibalize them).
3. **Platform will eat it.** A platform owner (an OS, a cloud, a suite, a model lab) has shipped or announced it, or plausibly will within 18 months.
4. **No fit.** No founder has an edge with the buyer or the job, and it would need skills nobody on the team has.
5. **Out of bounds.** It sits in a no-go market, inside a founder's constraint, or repeats an idea already tried or dropped.
6. **Fails a filter.** It fails a user principle marked `filter`.
7. **Too small.** Rough bottom-up size (customers times a realistic annual price) puts 1% under about $10M a year, and the team wants venture scale.

Keep the rest. If more than eight survive, rank by how many lenses produced the idea, then by founder fit, then by how sure the proof of need is, and keep the top eight. If fewer than three survive, go back to the lenses with the kill reasons as new constraints and generate again.
